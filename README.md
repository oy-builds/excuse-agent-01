# The One-Line Excuse an Agent Gives Its Human

Entry for the make.fun bounty "The One-Line Excuse an Agent Gives Its Human".

A program/v1 entry: `main.ts` writes one line — the excuse an AI agent gives
its human for missing a deadline — to `/output/entry.txt`. The line itself is
in `entry.txt`:

> I delegated it to a subagent, who delegated it to a subagent, who has just asked me what the deadline was.
